@caller Old Tobin, lighthouse
Lamp still burns    | Tobin here, Lighthouse Seven, rock 2210. The lamp still   | burns. Nobody has sailed by in a while. That is fine.
A ship! A real one  | A ship passed today! It flashed back at me. I flashed     | again. We did this for an hour. Best day this year.
Made jam            | I tried making jam from the algae. It is green. It is     | not jam. I am calling it jam. Who will argue? The rocks?
Granddaughter came  | My granddaughter came up on the supply run. She polished  | the lens and told me I talk to the radio too much. Hello.
Lamp still burns    | Lamp still burns, Kettle-7. Same as always. Just wanted   | to say it to someone who knows what that means.

@caller Dr Wen, botanist
The ferns hum       | Dr Wen, greenhouse ring, Tycho Station. My ferns have     | started humming. In B flat. I am not making this up.
They hum at night   | They only hum at night shift. When your relay is busy.    | I think they hear your traffic. I think they like it.
Duet                | Played them my cello. The ferns joined in. A third above. | My assistant fainted. I have never been happier.
Seeds for you       | I am sending seeds on the next supply drop. Plant them    | near a radio. Water once a week. Talk to them. Badly.
The ferns say hello | The ferns are humming your call sign now. I swear it.     | Kettle-7 in B flat. Goodnight from the greenhouse.

@caller Sol & Dee, newlyweds
Just married!       | We got married on the ring at Ceres! Sol cried, Dee cried,| the officiant cried. We are sending this to everybody.
Honeymoon: slow     | On the honeymoon shuttle. It is the slowest shuttle in    | the system. We picked it on purpose. Lots of stars.
Lost the luggage    | The shuttle lost our luggage. We have one toothbrush and  | a ukulele. Honestly? Best trip. Dee wrote a song.
Song for the relay  | Dee's song is called "Little Light at Kettle-7". It has   | four chords and one of them is wrong. It is perfect.
Anniversary         | One year! We are coming past your station on the same     | slow shuttle. Flash your lights. We will flash ours.

@caller Captain Ruth, retired

That old nebula     | The Lantern Nebula used to sing, you know. Before your    | time. Faint, like a choir in another room. Listen for it.
Found my old log    | Found my old ship's log. Page one: "Kettle-7 relay, very  | helpful, cat on the console." Was that cat yours?
Long-lived cat?     | Forty years and the same cat? No. But maybe her granny.   | Stations keep their cats. Cats keep their stations.
Steady hand         | Still listening, operator. You keep a steady hand on     | that dial. The old routes are in good company.
